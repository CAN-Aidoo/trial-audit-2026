# Email to corresponding authors

Send one email per paper, to the corresponding author named on the arXiv PDF. Paste that paper's sheet
from `authors/sheets/<id>.md` below the message. Log each email you send, and each reply, in
`authors/log.csv` (columns: id, sent, replied, outcome, notes).

---

**Subject:** Please check the numbers we extracted from "<paper title>" (arXiv <id>)

Dear Dr <name>,

I am auditing the statistics of real-robot evaluations in 2026 force-, tactile- and current-aware
manipulation papers. Your paper is one of the 58 we included. The audit looks at evaluation practice
across the field as a whole, not at any one paper:
- results are reported only in aggregate;
- papers are not ranked or named in the text.

Before the revision, I would be grateful if you could check the rows we extracted from your paper (below).
In particular, please check:
- the trial and success counts;
- whether each comparator is the right one for its role.

Any correction you send will be applied, and logged with your name or anonymously, as you prefer. If there
is nothing to correct, no reply is needed.

The protocol, data and code are public at <repository URL>, and the current draft is at <draft link>. If I can
I will include your corrections in the next revision.

Thank you for your time, and for releasing a paper we could read in this much detail.
